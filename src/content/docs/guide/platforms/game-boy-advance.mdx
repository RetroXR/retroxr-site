The Game Boy Advance plays cartridges from your `roms/gba` folder.

What it grew, and what is new in **v0.6.0**, is the
**[Card e-Reader](/guide/platforms/e-reader/)**, a cartridge with a slit through its roof
that you slide printed dotcode cards through, edge first. It has a page of its own, because
which cards it will read depends on how they are named.

## The handheld and its cartridges

Since v0.9.0 the Game Boy Advance wears a modeled shell, in **indigo** for now, and its
cartridges are modeled too. Neither carries a logo, which is a legal necessity rather than a
choice. A cartridge is gray unless its game came in another color, in which case RetroXR
picks that: the Pokémon cartridges come out in their own colors in **clear, frosted
plastic**, and the NES Classic series in gray. The label is your scraped label art, if the
scraper found one. See [box art](/guide/connecting/box-art/).

Hold a game's row in the spawn menu for a second to pick a different color yourself,
as described in [cartridges and discs](/guide/playing/cartridges-and-discs/).

## Linking

The GBA link cable chains up to four handhelds, does single-cartridge multiplayer, and
reaches a GameCube on the GC-GBA cable. All of it is on the [link
cables](/guide/playing/link-cables/) page.

Original Game Boy and Game Boy Color games have [a page of their
own](/guide/platforms/game-boy/).
