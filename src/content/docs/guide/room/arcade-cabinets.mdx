Arcade games do not come out as cartridges. They come out as the hardware they were sold
as, and they go into a cabinet:

| System | The game is | Its cabinet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MAME** and **Arcade** | a **JAMMA board** | the Morphing Arcade Cabinet |
| **DICE** | a **JAMMA board** | a simple arcade cabinet |
| **Neo Geo** | a **Neo Geo MVS cartridge** | a simple Neo Geo cabinet |
| **Laserdisc** | a disc | a simple arcade cabinet |

## Putting a game in

Each cabinet has a **bay** made of a disc reader, for MAME CHDs, and a **JAMMA docking
board**. Insert the board or cartridge, and the cabinet **skins itself from the game**:
its top and sides take the game's scraped art. See [box art](/guide/connecting/box-art/)
for scraping.

The simple cabinets have **two controller plugs**, which take any controller-type device.

## Opening a cabinet

A cabinet's doors swing on hinges, and you take each one by its handle:

- **In VR**, put a hand on the handle, pull the **trigger** and swing the door with your
  hand.
- **On desktop**, aim at the handle until the open-hand icon shows, hold the **left mouse
  button** (the icon becomes a fist) and roll the **scroll wheel**: up opens, down closes.

The Morphing Arcade Cabinet has two. The **coin panel** under the controls carries the coin
doors, and behind it is the **cash box**: every coin you put in drops into its tray. The
tray slides out on its rail with its coins in it, can be carried off, and goes back in. The
coins in it are saved with the room. The **bay door** below opens on the board. A board
only goes in or comes out while that door is open.

For games that read them, the bay also holds the **TEST** and **SERVICE** switches and an
operator's selector, for the game's own settings menu.

## Putting in coins

A coin is a credit, as on the real thing, and the coin doors are the only way in for one.

- **In VR**, bring a free hand to the slot. A token appears pinched at your fingertip; push
  it through the slot.
- **On desktop**, aim at the slot until the coin icon shows, and click. A token flies in
  from the corner of the view.

You hear it scrape in and land in the cash box, and that is the moment the game counts it.
Tokens are free and never run out, and loose ones (from the cash box) go into a slot the
same way. A coin put into a cabinet that is only showing its game starts the real one. The
slot's lamp blinks while the game is starting, and the coins are counted as soon as it is
up. The **coin return** button on the coin door gives back any coin the game has not
counted yet, into the door's return cup.

## The Morphing Arcade Cabinet

Arcades used all sorts of hardware beyond a stick and buttons. The Morphing Arcade
Cabinet reads MAME's lists and **rebuilds its controls for the game you insert**. Put in a
shooter and it grows guns; put in a racing game and it grows a wheel.

It knows guns, driving wheels, motorbikes, boards, dance pads, taiko drums, guitars, DJ
stations, a rifle with a scope, a plane cockpit and rolling balls. Some cabinets come with
a **racing seat**, and some of those seats move.

The shapes are approximations, not copies of the original cabinets. If a cabinet morphs
wrong for a game, or a game needs hardware it does not grow, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/RetroXR/RetroXR/issues). And if you are a 3D artist,
contributions to make the cabinets more faithful are very welcome.

## Attract mode

A switched-on cabinet plays the game's **scraped video**, the way a real cabinet loops its
attract sequence. The emulator is not running yet, which keeps a room full of cabinets
cheap. It starts when you grab a controller, press a button or insert a coin.

Attract mode can be turned off on a single cabinet's menu, or for every cabinet in
`OPTIONS`.

## Using the screen for something else

Every cabinet has a **component input**, so any other console can be cabled to it and
played on the cabinet's screen. The input is bypassed while the JAMMA or Neo Geo bay has a
game in it, so take the game out to use it.

## Sega's NAOMI cabinets

Four of Sega's own cabinets are on [mod.io](/guide/room/mods/): **Sega Blast City**,
**Sega Aero City**, **Sega New Net City** and **Sega NAOMI Universal**. Once installed they
are on the **Naomi** and **Arcade** cards in the spawn menu. Each comes with a **NAOMI
board** in its bay, cabled up the way it would leave the factory. Its game goes on the
board: a NAOMI cartridge, or the DIMM board with its key chip and a GD-ROM drive cabled to
it, with the disc in the drive. More mods on mod.io give the NAOMI, the JVS boards and the
GD-ROM set their real looks; without them RetroXR draws its own stand-ins.

A NAOMI is a board on its own, and every cord between it and the cabinet does a job. Take
one out and that part of the cabinet stops, as it would on the real hardware:

| Cord | From the NAOMI to | Without it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Power** | the cabinet's supply | nothing works |
| **VGA** | the monitor | a black screen |
| **Audio** (two phono plugs) | the amplifier | silence, one channel per plug |
| **JVS** (a USB cable) | the cabinet's I/O board, or the converter | the controls and the coin slots do nothing |

### JVS cabinets: New Net City and NAOMI Universal

These were made for the NAOMI. Their controls and coin switches are wired to a **JVS I/O
board**, and the JVS cable runs from the NAOMI's USB-A socket to the I/O board's USB-B. On
New Net City the board is in the base, behind the rear hatch.

### JAMMA cabinets: Blast City and Aero City

These are older JAMMA cabinets, wired to a **JAMMA harness** rather than an I/O board. A
NAOMI goes into one through a **JVS-to-JAMMA converter**, as it did in an arcade: the
NAOMI's JVS cable goes into the converter, and the cabinet's JAMMA harness goes on the
converter's edge. The VGA cable can go to the monitor or into the converter. On Blast City
the converter stands on the floor behind the front left door, under the board's shelf, with
its JVS socket at the back.

## Somewhere to sit

A chair, a stool, a cushion, a sofa and a bed can all be spawned from `SPAWN` → **Objects**,
whether or not there is a cabinet to sit at.
