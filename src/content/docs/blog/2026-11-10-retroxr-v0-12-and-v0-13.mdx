## 🕹️ Arcade cabinets (*v0.13*)

The Arcade and Neo Geo systems were reworked to look more like the real thing. Arcade and
DICE games now come out as **JAMMA boards** rather than generic cartridges, Neo Geo games
as **MVS cartridges**, and they go into cabinets:

- The **Morphing Arcade Cabinet**, for MAME and Arcade games
- Simpler cabinets for the **Neo Geo**, **DICE** and **Laserdisc** systems

A cabinet skins itself from the game inside it, using the scraped art for its top and
sides. Its bay holds a disc reader for MAME CHDs and a JAMMA docking board, and the simple
cabinet has two controller plugs that take any controller-type device.

### It morphs

The Morphing Arcade Cabinet reads MAME's lists and **rebuilds its hardware for the game
you insert**. Put in a shooter and it grows guns; put in a racer and it grows a wheel. It
also knows motorbikes, boards, dance pads, taiko drums, guitars, DJ stations, a rifle with
a scope, a plane cockpit and rolling balls. Some cabinets get racing seats, and some of
those seats move.

That should cover all the non-standard hardware arcades ever used, and every combination
was tested, but some may have slipped through. If a cabinet morphs wrong or is missing
something, please [open an issue](https://github.com/RetroXR/RetroXR/issues). The shapes
are approximations of the real cabinets, and 3D artists who want to make them better are
very welcome.

### Attract mode and a component input

A switched-on cabinet plays the game's **scraped video** until someone grabs a controller,
presses a button or drops a coin. Only then does the emulator start, which keeps an arcade
full of cabinets cheap to run. Attract mode can be turned off per cabinet or in
`OPTIONS`.

Each cabinet also has a **component input**, so any other console can use its screen.
It is bypassed while the JAMMA or Neo Geo bay has a game in it.

## 🔌 Power (*v0.12, v0.13*)

v0.12 laid the groundwork: **regional wall outlets** (pick yours in `OPTIONS` → General
and the room's sockets and plugs change to match), AA and AAA batteries, and battery
covers.

v0.13 makes it do something. Turn on **Options → General → Enable Power Simulation**
and devices only run when power actually reaches them. Handhelds take **AC adapters** in
their DC IN, as do the 32X, Sega CD, Disk System drive and Game Boy Printer. Each region
has its own power strip, and there is a universal travel adapter too. The original Game
Boy gets **C batteries** and the **DMG-05 Battery Case**. Every powered object's menu says
what it runs on.

<Aside type="note" title="It is opt-in">
	Power simulation is **off by default**. With it off, nothing needs plugging in. With it
	on, a room that has no wall outlet still powers everything.
</Aside>

## 💿 New cores (*v0.13*)

Download them from the **BIOS / Extras** view in `CORES`. New platforms get their own
tiles and icons.

- **Sony PlayStation 3** (RPCS3). `PS3UPDAT.PUP` goes in `system/rpcs3/`, and a game can
  be a folder dump.
- **Microsoft Xbox 360** (Xenia Edge)
- **Sony PlayStation Vita** (Vita3K)
- **Sony PlayStation 2** (ARMSX2), Quest only
- **Sony PocketStation** (Pokket)
- **Nintendo 64** (n64emu), Windows only
- **Sega Dreamcast / Naomi** (ParaLLEl DC)
- **Sega Hikaru** (Odin) and **Sega Lindbergh** (linuxloader), Windows only
- **Daphne** laserdisc arcade games (HypseusHW)
- **MSX, MSX2, MSX2+ and turbo R** (openMSX)
- **Fujitsu FM Towns** (Geartowns)
- **Atari ST, STE, TT and Falcon** (hatariB, Hatari 2014)
- **Playdia**, **HyperScan**, **GameTank**, **Wenquxing** and **Coolbar**
- **DOS** (rust-dos), **Doom, Heretic, Hexen and Strife** (UZDoom), **Duke Nukem 3D**
  (EDuke32)
- **Internet radio**

mGBA, Gambatte, PCSX ReARMed and Flycast were all updated as well. Each core has a
**Download all** button on the BIOS page now. The PS3 and Xbox 360 cores are offered on
Quest too, to try, and on Quest the PS2 tile recommends Play! and hides PCSX2.

Some cores the libretro buildbot does not build yet (ParaLLEl GameCube/Wii, PSP and Xbox
among them) already have their info in RetroXR, so they will show up in the list as soon
as the buildbot builds them.

## New hardware (*v0.13*)

### 🦔 Sonic & Knuckles

The lock-on cartridge, spawned from the Genesis card. Put another Genesis cartridge in the
slot on its top. It needs `sk.bin` **and** `sk2chip.bin` in genesis_plus_gx's system
folder, and it also fits a 32X. See [Sega Genesis](/guide/platforms/sega-genesis/).

### 🐾 The PocketStation

Spawned from the PlayStation card: a memory card that is also a handheld, built from
Sony's own measurements. Out of the console it runs on Pokket, and what you save on it
goes back to its card. Docked in a PlayStation running PCSX ReARMed, **it is a real
PocketStation to the game**, so games can download their minigames to it, and its screen
shows and is kept in savestates. It needs `system/pokket/pocketstation.bin`. See
[PlayStation](/guide/platforms/playstation/#the-pocketstation).

### 👥 The Game Boy four-player adapter

The **DMG-07** is a core and an object in the room. Plug up to four Game Boys into it, on
Gambatte or mGBA, for four-player link games. It is powered as long as player 1's plug is
in a Game Boy. Download it from **BIOS / Extras** under Game Boy. See
[Game Boy](/guide/platforms/game-boy/#four-players-the-dmg-07).

### More

- **The Game Boy Player**, spawned from the GameCube card. A seated Game Pak can be pulled
  straight out by hand.
- **The SNES Super Scope**, with its infrared receiver and sight, on snes9x. The receiver
  goes in the second controller port, and the scope needs batteries, its switch on, and a
  clear line of sight. See [light guns](/guide/playing/light-guns/#the-super-scope).
- **Multiplayer adapters**: the NES Four Score, the NES Satellite, the Famicom 4 Players
  Adaptor, and the PlayStation and PlayStation 2 Multitaps.
- **The 32X is wired like the real one.** The Genesis's picture now goes through it: run
  the Video Link Cable from the Genesis's A/V OUT to the 32X's A/V IN, and the TV from the
  32X's A/V OUT. That closes the known issue from the last post.
- **The Sega CD Model 2** (a stand-in for now), and the Model 1 Genesis's 8-pin A/V OUT
  with its lead and the 32X Conversion Cable.
- **Console stands** for the Wii and the PS2.
- **Expansions that need a BIOS are always spawnable.** They used to stay hidden until the
  firmware was installed. Now they show a red **BIOS missing** line, and switching one on
  without it tells you which file is missing.

## 📺 A new shelf of appliances (*v0.13*)

New **RETRO XR** models with live displays and buttons you press with a fingertip, the
laser or the mouse: a **TV** (now the spawn menu's default, with a push-push key door), a
26-key **universal remote**, a **vinyl player** with an RCA line out, a **cassette player**
and a **CD player** with working headphone jacks, a **surround amp**, a **DVD player** and
a **VCR**.

There is also an **AV switch** with four composite inputs and one output to the TV. Click a
number to switch, click the slide's tab to step, or drag it.

Cassettes and LPs now have **two sides**. Put a `#SIDE B` line in the album's `.m3u`
before the first track of side B, then flip the tape or the record to play it. See
[music](/guide/room/music/#side-a-and-side-b).

The TV gained **IPTV channels from M3U playlists** and a 90s volume display. Handhelds and
TVs have headphone jacks, and there are RETRO XR headphones to plug into them.

## 🧩 Modding (*v0.12, v0.13*)

- **Any labeled mod prop can be spawned** from `SPAWN` → Objects. Before, a mod prop that
  was not a controller, a gun or a lead could not be spawned at all.
- **Mod platforms** get a Games tile, and can declare their BIOS and pin core options.
- New calls for dressing the Morphing Arcade Cabinet, adding AC adapters and battery
  labels, and adding console stands, plus hooks for remote control, menu rows and
  fingertip buttons.
- Installed mods show their version. v0.12 can install and enable mods automatically.
- **Netplay** tells a peer that was refused over its mods which ones it is missing, and
  lets it download them from mod.io.

v0.12 also changed `RcaPort` and `LinkPort` to extend `Port`, so mods built on the old
classes need updating. See [mods](/guide/room/mods/) and the
[modding guide](https://github.com/RetroXR/RetroXR/blob/master/docs/modding.md).

## 👓 Passthrough that knows your room (*v0.12*, Quest only)

Passthrough can now use Meta's room data: furniture mode by default, or the full room mesh
on a Quest 3. Quest asks for permission the first time you enter passthrough. The libretro
camera can use Meta's depth too, so the **Game Boy Camera works in passthrough**, as long
as what you point it at is visible in passthrough. The options are on the General ribbon.

## Smaller things worth knowing

- **Press any button with a fingertip**: every button on every controller, handheld, VMU,
  Nunchuk and light gun. D-pads take a press on each arm. Turn it off with **Press
  Controller Buttons by Touch** in `OPTIONS`.
- **The bedroom has a street outside**: swaying trees, lit windows and porch lamps, new
  blinds with a working tilt wand, and a three-gang switch that also runs the ceiling fan.
  On a headset its lighting now follows the time-of-day lever and the blinds.
- **A welcome page** at startup, with a console hookup clip and a Quest upload guide. It can
  be turned off.
- **Searching Games** now covers every game, by system name and by regional title.
- **Menus** can be resized, warn about crossed cords, and have a Debug tab on every machine.
  **SCENE → Objects** finds or deletes what you have spawned, and the SCENE cards now show
  pictures.
- **Chairs**: a chair, stool, cushion, sofa and bed, all freely spawnable.
- **Desktop** runs faster on Windows, and a second laser no longer drags the frame rate down.
- **The Game Boy Printer** (v0.12) has more accurate motor sounds, paper that can run as long
  as you like, thermal paper in the official colors, and paper physics.
- **Deleting things** (v0.12): drag an item onto the trash can in the spawn menu, or use the
  delete button on its menu.
- Game Boy LCD settings, handheld screens lit by the room, posters up to 8x, and folder
  creation in the Web File Manager, which also explains why a ROM is not showing up.

<Aside type="caution" title="Stream channels in channels.json are gone">
	Hand-typed stream entries in `channels.json` were removed in favor of M3U playlists. Put
	a `.m3u` in the `tv` folder of your data root, or add an M3U URL on the antenna's menu.
	Old entries are ignored and logged but left in the file. HDHomeRun and M3U sources in
	`channels.json` still work. See [the television](/guide/room/television/#iptv-channels).
</Aside>

## Fixes

Objects no longer hang in the air after you let go (the known issue from the last post),
or jitter while moving in VR. VLC audio no longer plays slow and flat on devices whose
sample rate differs from the engine's, which affected VHS, DVD, CD and TV on Quest. Book
pages stop clipping, the Systems tab no longer freezes on Quest, and a netplay joiner no
longer times out before the host's room loads.

## Known issues

- Some cores for later generations may struggle on Quest.
- Netplay is still experimental.
