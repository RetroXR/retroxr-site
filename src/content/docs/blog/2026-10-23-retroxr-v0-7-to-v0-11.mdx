The devlog fell behind the releases. v0.7.0, v0.8.0, v0.9.0, v0.10.0 and v0.11.0 — plus the
v0.11.1 hotfix — all shipped without a post, so this one rounds them up by theme rather than
by version. Each section points at the guide page that now covers it properly.

## New ways to play

### 🎴 The Dreamcast VMU — *v0.7, v0.9*

The VMU is a memory card with a screen, and in RetroXR it is both. Saves go on it, its
screen shows what the game sends it, and it **plays its own games**: put them in
`roms/vmu` and start them from the VMU's menu with its Play button. Since v0.9 it takes the
English or Japanese BIOS and keeps save states. The controller dongle now has both
expansion slots, and a **Jump Pack** gives the pad rumble. See
[Dreamcast](/guide/platforms/dreamcast/).

### 🖥️ Focus Mode and full screen — *v0.7*

Sometimes you want the game, not the room. Point at a TV or handheld and **click the right
stick** in VR, or press **F11** on the desktop. In VR the picture fills your view over a
black void or passthrough, with the console's controllers still in reach; hold grip on the
screen to drag it, and use the stick while holding to resize it or change its depth. See
[VR controls](/guide/reference/vr-controls/) and
[desktop controls](/guide/reference/desktop-controls/).

### 🔊 5.1 surround — *v0.8, v0.10*

The TV grew RCA outputs for front left and right, centre, sub and the two surrounds, plus
a setting choosing between its own speakers and external stereo or surround. Stand the
speakers where you sit, run the wires, and games that offer **Dolby Surround or Pro Logic
II** decode properly — pick SURROUND in a game like Donkey Kong 64. v0.10 added an
amplifier that takes a stereo signal and does the decoding itself. Spatial Audio has to be
on. See [speakers and surround](/guide/playing/plugging-in/).

### 🎤 Microphones — *v0.8*

Your headset's microphone is now a controller accessory: the N64 **Voice Recognition Unit**
for Hey You, Pikachu! (with a speech pack to download), the GameCube microphone in memory
card slot 2 for Mario Party 6, the Dreamcast microphone, and the Famicom's. See
[controllers](/guide/playing/controllers/).

### 🖨️ The Game Boy Printer and Camera — *v0.10, v0.11*

Cable a Game Boy to the **Game Boy Printer** and print; tear the paper off and the print is
saved as a PNG in the `prints/` folder. The **Game Boy Camera** (on mGBA) photographs the
room you are standing in. Each has its own page:
[Game Boy Printer](/guide/platforms/game-boy-printer/) and
[Game Boy Camera](/guide/platforms/game-boy-camera/).

v0.11.1 also made the original Game Boy a real DMG — a green LCD, and dual-mode Game Boy
Color cartridges play in it — while the Game Boy Color colours original games with its own
built-in palettes. See [Game Boy](/guide/platforms/game-boy/).

## Link cables, everywhere — *v0.9, v0.10, v0.11*

The link bus that started with Game Boys and GameCubes now carries far more:

- **PlayStation 2 i.LINK**, up to six consoles through the hub
- The **Sega Saturn** link cable and the **Game Gear**'s Gear-to-Gear
- **Atari Lynx** ComLynx, the **Neo Geo Pocket** and **WonderSwan** cables, the
  **Atari Jaguar** JagLink, and the **Virtual Boy** link
- **Game Boy Color infrared** — the window glows red when two are talking
- **LAN** through a Network Adapter, for the PlayStation 2 and the GameCube

See [link cables](/guide/playing/link-cables/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Linking over netplay needs v0.11.1 everywhere">
	v0.11.1 renamed the link protocols, so every player in a linked netplay session has to be
	on v0.11.1 with updated cores. v0.11.0's own builds would not plug link cables in at
	all — that is what the hotfix was for.
</Aside>

Rollback netplay also got serialisation fixes in a long list of cores — see
[netplay](/guide/connecting/netplay/).

## Hardware with real shells — *v0.7, v0.8, v0.9*

The grey boxes are giving way. New models include the **Dreamcast**, **Genesis 2** and its
cartridge, the **32X** and its cartridge, the **Game Boy Advance** and **e-Reader**, the
**Nintendo 64DD** and its disks, and cartridges for nearly everything: NES (redone, at the
proper thickness), SNES in Type A, Type B, PAL and Japanese shapes, N64 in Japanese and
US/European shapes, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, GBA, DS and 3DS.

Cartridges **pick their own colour** where the real one was special — gold Zelda carts,
Pokémon carts, Killer Instinct — and the right regional shape from the scraped region and
date. Hold a cartridge for a second as you spawn it to choose the shape or any colour you
like. See [cartridges and discs](/guide/playing/cartridges-and-discs/).

Trademarks had to come off the models for legal reasons.

## Mods — *v0.10, v0.11*

There is now an in-game **Mod Browser**, backed by [mod.io](https://mod.io/g/retroxr),
with an initial set of models to download and update from inside the headset. See
[mods](/guide/room/mods/).

## More places to run it

Releases now ship for **Meta Quest** (2, 3 and Pro) and **Windows** — desktop and PCVR —
plus experimental **Linux** (x86_64 and arm64) and **macOS** builds. See
[download & install](/download/).

## Smaller things worth knowing

- **Saves are kept per system, not per core** — the N64, 64DD and Famicom Disk System
  cores are the exceptions for now — and the folders were moved for you on update. ROM
  folders were renamed at the same time (`snes`, `psx`, `n64`…); the old names still work.
  The Sega CD, Saturn and 3DO save inside the console like the real ones, with backup carts
  to move saves around. See [saves](/guide/connecting/saves/).
- **PlayStation 2 memory cards** in both slots, with a save viewer that renders the 3D
  icons. See [PlayStation 2](/guide/platforms/playstation-2/).
- **Saturn carts**: 1 MB and 4 MB RAM, backup RAM, and the MPEG cart. See
  [Sega Saturn](/guide/platforms/sega-saturn/).
- **Scraping**: Scrape All, faster multithreaded scrapes when signed in to ScreenScraper,
  auto-accepted results, easier region and language priority, and region flags and disc
  numbers in the game lists. See [box art](/guide/connecting/box-art/).
- **RomM** logins by username and password fetch the token for you.
- **The live TV tuner** is no longer built into the TV — spawn the antenna and cable it
  in. See [television](/guide/room/television/).
- **Books** flop more naturally, turn with Q and E (tap quickly to riffle), and scale down
  to a tenth of their size. See [books](/guide/room/books/).
- **Music**: `.m3u` playlists define records and tapes, and both show label art.
- **Desktop**: mouse sensitivity, resolution and zoom options.
- **Cores**: Xemu for the original Xbox on Quest and Windows; the Wii Menu, the DS home
  screen and the GameCube IPL can all be booted with their BIOS in place. See
  [BIOS files](/guide/cores/bios/).
- **The log** opens with your version and platform, explains why a held object won't seat,
  and can be read from the Web File Manager on Quest. See
  [reporting bugs](/guide/reference/reporting-bugs/).

## Known issues

- Objects can occasionally float after being dropped. Pick them up and drop them again.
- A link cable does not plug into the e-Reader's link port.
- A 32X still needs its A/V lead plugged into the Genesis.
- ScreenScraper often lacks the top-fold label for cartridges, and returns no proper label
  texture for 64DD disks.
- The 64DD's access lamp is barely documented, so it may not behave exactly like the real
  one.
